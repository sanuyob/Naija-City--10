# Naija City – downloaded street asset library

Source: Grab3D, Urban Street Kit / Street & City models
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication). Each original asset's LICENSE.txt is stored beside the source GLB.

Source model pages:
- Utility Pole: https://grab3d.com/models/city/utility-pole/
- Street Light: https://grab3d.com/models/city/street-light/
- Bus Stop Shelter: https://grab3d.com/models/city/bus-stop-shelter/
- Billboard: https://grab3d.com/models/city/billboard/
- Curb and Sidewalk: https://grab3d.com/models/city/curb-sidewalk-segment/
- Dumpster: https://grab3d.com/models/city/dumpster/
- Bollard: https://grab3d.com/models/city/bollard/
- Advertising Column: https://grab3d.com/models/city/advertising-column/

Original files are kept in this folder. `mobile/` contains copies resized to a maximum 512px texture dimension for lower memory use. Geometry has not been simplified. The assets are downloaded, not yet wired into district spawning. Before use in repeated placements, inspect scale, materials, draw calls, and actual Android performance. Some models depict generic Western street furniture and should be selectively adapted or omitted where they do not fit a Nigerian setting.
