# Naija City — Yaba Nigerian Street Life Asset Library

This is a curated source library for Yaba's street businesses, market activity, small retail, food vending, power interruptions and signage. The downloaded models are candidate assets, not yet placed in the live world. Originals are preserved in `original/`; phone-oriented copies with textures capped at 512px are in `mobile/`.

## Custom project assets

- `custom/overhead-wire-span.glb` is an original Naija City mesh generated by `scripts/generate-yaba-overhead-wire-span.mjs`. It is a 12 m span with three dark insulated sagging cables and about 1,176 triangles. It contains no utility poles; place between suitable pole tops. It is not a downloaded model.
- `../signage/` contains eight original 1024×512 Nigerian street-business sign textures generated by `scripts/generate-yaba-signage-textures.mjs`. Names include Yaba Provisions, Mide Phone Repairs, Fresh Cut Barbers, Mama T's Kitchen, Akara & Pap, Suya & Grill, POS & Transfers, and Cold Drinks. These are original project graphics, not third-party logos.

## Adaptation and performance notes

Several downloaded models have a stylized or non-Nigerian visual language. Use them as donor models where appropriate: recolour signs and awnings, replace display goods, remove foreign pictograms, and use the market-stall/cart structures as bases for local sellers. The shopfront, bakery and corner-shop buildings need a style and scale review before they become Yaba buildings. The ramen/hot-dog carts should be redressed as Nigerian roadside food vending, not copied literally.

The 512px copies reduce texture memory, but they do not simplify geometry or guarantee a frame-rate improvement. Avoid placing every unique model in view at once; reuse a few assets with varied transforms/material tints, batch repeated details where practical, and verify draw calls and performance on the target Android phone. These files are not yet connected to runtime spawning or district streaming.

There was no suitable ready-made overhead cable span in the checked Grab3D catalogue, so a small reusable project-authored wire-span GLB was created instead.
