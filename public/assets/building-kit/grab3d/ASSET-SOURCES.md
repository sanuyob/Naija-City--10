Originals are preserved in `original/`; `mobile/` variants have textures resized to a maximum 512px. Geometry is unchanged. These are candidate assets only, not runtime-integrated. The Grab3D packs have a stylized/cozy visual direction, so inspect and selectively recolor or use façade components before placing them in the more grounded Nigerian districts. Do not replace existing authored landmark buildings without a visual and scale review.
